Our story

Heirloom shapes in honest silver

Kira began with the jewellery passed down through our family: temple pieces, kundan rings and heavy silver kept for weddings and festivals. We wanted pieces with that same weight and meaning that you could wear every day.

Every Kira design starts from a traditional Indian form. We make it in solid 925 sterling silver, never plated brass. Our gold-tone pieces are gold finished over that same silver base, so every piece lasts long enough to be handed down again.

How a piece is made

From sketch to hallmark

  1. Design

    Each piece starts as a sketch drawn from temple carvings, jaali screens and family heirlooms.

  2. Cast & shape

    Silversmiths cast, hammer and solder the sterling silver, then add each bead and bell by hand.

  3. Finish

    A mirror polish, an oxidised wash that settles into the carving, or a gold-tone finish over the silver.

  4. Stamp & check

    Every piece is stamped 925 and checked for purity before it is packed in its pouch.

Silver care

Keep it bright for decades

Sterling silver darkens naturally when it meets air and moisture. A little care keeps it looking new.

Wear it often
The natural oils in your skin keep silver from tarnishing. Pieces kept in a drawer darken faster than pieces you wear.
Last on, first off
Put jewellery on after perfume, lotion and hairspray. Take it off before swimming, bathing or the gym.
Store it sealed
Keep each piece in its Kira pouch or a zip bag, with a strip of chalk or a silica packet to absorb moisture.
Clean gently
Wipe with a soft silver cloth. On oxidised pieces, polish only the raised areas so the dark finish stays in the grooves. On gold-tone pieces, wipe with a dry soft cloth only.
